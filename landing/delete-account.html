Account Deletion

Delete your Mood8 account.

You can permanently delete your Mood8 account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains the two methods, what gets removed, and what may be retained by law.

App: Mood8 Developer: Mood8 Support: support@mood8.app

01 How to delete your account

Choose either method. Both remove your account.

1

In the Mood8 app

The fastest way — takes about 15 seconds.

  1. Open Mood8 on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete account (shown in red).
  5. Read the confirmation screen. If you have an active Premium subscription, the screen tells you it will be canceled as part of the deletion.
  6. Type DELETE to confirm you understand this is permanent.
  7. Tap Delete my account. The app signs out to the welcome screen.
2

By email

If you can't access the app, or you'd rather do it manually.

  1. Send an email to support@mood8.app from the email address on your Mood8 account. (Sending from the account address lets us verify you own it.)
  2. Subject line: “Delete my Mood8 account”. Body can be a single line — no reason required.
  3. We reply to confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually the same business day.

02 What is deleted

Deleting your account removes the following from our servers:

  • Account: email address, name, profile photo, bio, hashed password (or Google Sign-In link).
  • Habits + completion history — every habit you tracked and every check-off.
  • Mood, energy, and focus check-ins across your whole history.
  • Gratitude entries and morning intentions.
  • AI Coach conversations — every message you sent and every reply from the Coach.
  • Challenge participation — your join history, check-ins, badges, and rank progression across every challenge.
  • Comments you posted inside challenges.
  • Notification and reminder settings — reminders you configured and the times they were set for.
  • All synced Mood8 data that lived on our servers to keep your devices in sync.

Challenges you created

If you created a challenge that other people joined, the challenge itself stays alive so other participants don't lose their progress. Your personal link to it is removed — the challenge shows the creator as [deleted user]. If you want the challenge itself taken down, mention that in your email request.

03 What may be retained, and for how long

A few small things may persist for legal or operational reasons:

  • Payment records (Stripe). If you subscribed to Premium, the payment / invoice records for those transactions are retained by Stripe for as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically several years). Mood8 doesn't store card numbers — those live only at Stripe. Any active Premium subscription is canceled as part of the deletion so no further charges occur.
  • Server backups. Our rolling encrypted backups may still contain your data for up to 30 days after the deletion, after which they're irretrievably overwritten as the backup window rolls forward.
  • Anonymous aggregate statistics. Numbers like "how many habits were completed on the platform this week" may remain in aggregated form. No personal data is retained in these; you can't be re-identified from them.
No shadow copies. Once deletion completes we do NOT keep a hidden profile of you for advertising, ranking, or any other purpose. Mood8 does not sell personal data and does not integrate advertising networks.

04 Timeline

Deletion from active systems is immediate for the in-app method — the API deletes the row and its children in the same request. Email requests are processed within 30 days, usually the same business day. Backup roll-off completes within another ~30 days after that.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your habits, streaks, check-ins, and history are unrecoverable after the request completes. If you're not sure, sign out and take a break instead — the account will still be here.
